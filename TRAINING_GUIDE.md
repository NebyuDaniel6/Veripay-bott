# VeriPay Training Guide

## 🍽️ **For Restaurant Owners/Admins**

### **Getting Started**
1. **Register your restaurant** by messaging the VeriPay bot
2. **Wait for Super Admin approval** (usually within 24 hours)
3. **Once approved**, you'll receive admin access

### **Managing Your Restaurant**
- **Approve waiters** who want to join your restaurant
- **View all transactions** from your restaurant
- **Upload bank statements** for reconciliation
- **Export reports** (CSV files) for accounting

### **Key Commands**
- `/start` - Begin using the bot
- `/menu` - Access main menu
- `/export_csv` - Download transaction data
- `/export_analytics` - Get performance reports

### **What You Can Do**
✅ **Approve new waiters** joining your restaurant  
✅ **View all payment transactions** from your restaurant  
✅ **Upload bank statements** for automatic reconciliation  
✅ **Export data** to Excel/Google Sheets  
✅ **Track waiter performance** and payment success rates  

---

## 👨‍🍳 **For Waiters**

### **Getting Started**
1. **Register as a waiter** by messaging the VeriPay bot
2. **Select your restaurant** from the list
3. **Wait for restaurant admin approval**
4. **Start processing payments** once approved

### **Processing Payments**
1. **Take a photo** of the customer's payment receipt
2. **Send the photo** to the VeriPay bot
3. **Bot automatically extracts** payment details
4. **Confirm the details** are correct
5. **Payment is recorded** in the system

### **Key Commands**
- `/start` - Begin using the bot
- `/menu` - Access main menu
- **Send photo** - Process payment receipt

### **What You Can Do**
✅ **Process payment receipts** by taking photos  
✅ **View your transaction history**  
✅ **Track your performance** and payment success  
✅ **Get instant confirmation** when payments are recorded  

---

## 📱 **How to Use the Bot**

### **Step 1: Start the Bot**
- Search for **@VeriPayBot** on Telegram
- Click **"Start"** or send `/start`

### **Step 2: Choose Your Role**
- **Restaurant Owner** → Register your restaurant
- **Waiter** → Register as a waiter

### **Step 3: Wait for Approval**
- **Restaurants**: Wait for Super Admin approval
- **Waiters**: Wait for Restaurant Admin approval

### **Step 4: Start Using**
- **Restaurants**: Manage waiters, view reports, export data
- **Waiters**: Process payment receipts by taking photos

---

## 🔧 **Troubleshooting**

### **Common Issues**
- **"Access denied"** → You need approval first
- **"OCR not working"** → Make sure receipt photo is clear
- **"Can't find restaurant"** → Restaurant needs to be approved first

### **Getting Help**
- Use the **"❓ Help"** button in the bot
- Contact your restaurant admin for waiter issues
- Contact Super Admin for restaurant issues
